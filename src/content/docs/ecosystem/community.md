## The Standard

The DVGE ecosystem relies on a clear taxonomy. Under the Manifest architecture, there are now three distinct layers:

1. **Templates**: Final, ready-to-render motion graphic animations (Overlays, Lower Thirds, Stingers). Ideal for immediate use by video editors and broadcasters.
2. **Tools**: Development assistance tools and internal utilities.
3. **Extensions**: Deep modules that expand the core capabilities of the rendering engine.

## The Official Catalog

The official catalog is a public repository where all code is open source (MIT) and auditable. Currently, it features plugins developed by the engine's author, and it is open to external contributions via Pull Requests.

## Commercial Freedom

DVGE is your platform; **you decide how to monetize your work**.

Agencies, freelancers, and creators are 100% free to create, obfuscate, and sell their own *Templates* and *Extensions* externally or privately. There are no artificial restrictions or vendor lock-in on what you develop on top of our technology.

## The Community Hub

The central gathering place is the **GitHub Discussions** of the main engine repository. We use it as a forum to:
- **Showcase**: Share your broadcast templates and custom plugins.
- **Ideas**: Propose new features for the rendering engine.
- **Support**: Get technical help and debug plugin issues with other developers.
